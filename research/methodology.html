Methodology & Limitations

How this site was built, and what it cannot claim

This page is the site's honesty anchor. Everything below is also true of the numbers on every other page.

Corpus story

1,640 papers collected (SSC 882 + Railway 758) across 19 named SSC and Railway examinations, free sources only, no login/payment/personal-data entry anywhere. 1,202 papers tagged in parcels (30,119 records), reconciled with zero-record, source-defective and alternate-print papers. Final merge: 40,894 records (34,942 certain + 5,952 uncertain) from 1,620 distinct papers after the 02-Feb-2026 Group D gapfill. The Master Report was written on the 34,917-certain / 1,619-paper base; the live corpus is 34,942 / 1,620. Report figures were not silently rewritten — both bases are labelled wherever they appear.

Four-way paper-type split (certain base): official 31,727 · bilingual-official 1,377 (together 94.7%) · similar 1,355 · memory-based 483. A paper is named from its own header, never its filename; a memory-based paper is never passed off as official; a missing shift stays missing.

Held-year rule: years use the exam's held cycle, not the notification year (e.g. NTPC 2025 cycle held 2026; Group D CEN 08/2024 held Nov 2025–Feb 2026). Shift rule: from headers/slots only (Group D 2025–26: 9:00 = S1, 12:45 = S2, 4:30 = S3; 2022 evening 5:00 PM = S3); shift null means unknown, never guessed. Thin-data flag: any cut with n < 30 certain records carries a thin-data flag; no verdict is drawn from it alone.

The 19 / 18 / 20 count story

The Master Report names 19 exams because it counts NTPC Graduate and NTPC Undergraduate separately. Tagging records carry a single NTPC code (post/stream not recorded), so this site lists 18 record-code exams. The library's 20th grouping is the NTPC 2020–21 combined cycle. NTPC cycle notes in the records: 5,359 rows without a cycle note, 1,631 rows noted "NTPC 2025 cycle, held 2026". Nothing here is a contradiction — it is one corpus counted three honest ways, and each page says which way it uses.

Copyright rule (binding)

Tagging records contain only paper filename + question number + tags — never question text, options or passages. Downloaded PDFs are delivered as files, never reproduced as text. This site follows the same rule: the library is paper-level and points to the source PDF. Publishing full question text would require an explicit overrule of this rule by the user; it has not been given and is not assumed.

What the data does NOT contain (shown as "Data unavailable", never invented)

  • Candidate accuracy / performance data — mistake analysis is error-sensitivity only.
  • Solving-time data — no per-question or per-topic times were measured.
  • Marks-share per topic — marking schemes changed across eras; a per-cycle scheme table would be needed first.
  • Per-exam official syllabus documents, exam-pattern tables, total-marks tables — never collected (syllabus overlap exists only as a Phase 1 tiers matrix).
  • A model/pattern/formula inventory as structured data — the records carry question types, model labels, shortcut labels and concept tags; anything beyond those fields is unavailable.
  • Per-formula PYQ counts — formulas come from hierarchy concepts; their PYQ usage is the topic's count, not a per-formula count.

Frozen corpus — deferred gaps (user: "baad mein")

The corpus is frozen at 34,942 certain records. Still open, deliberately untouched until the user reopens them: JE scope choice (2014+2015+2026 / 2026-only / none); CGL 2011 add-or-not; mixed-language English-subset recovery (13 quarantined Group D files); 6 Group D shifts with no usable file (27-Dec S1/S2, 13-Aug S1/S3, 14-Aug S2, 18-Aug S2); remaining E1/MI/CHSL gaps. None of these is quietly covered by a substitute anywhere on this site.

Out of scope (explicitly)

State boards, banking, insurance, defence, other central exams, UPSC, JPSC. No pages exist for them, and none will be invented.

Known data quirks (named, not hidden)

  • DI 2025 = 10 records, zero from CHSL/CPO/MTS (vs 271 in 2024) — a coverage/under-tagging flag for image-heavy DI sets, NOT a syllabus verdict. DI's CIS rank (13th) is understated until resolved.
  • CPO Tier-1 12-Dec-2019 Shift 1 is source-defective (stem-less, tick/cross icons only) — listed as missing, never substituted.
  • Secondary-topic pairings cover only 769 certain records (2.2%) — pairing counts are subsample counts.
  • 3 disputed records are flagged, never silently corrected: Group D 2018-09-24 S1 Q18 (Geometry) + Q26 (Trigonometry); MI 2015-08-30 S2 Q04 (TSD).
  • Number System ↔ HCF-LCM form a mutual-dependency cycle — co-foundations, studied together first.
  • Algebra's 2022 spike (11.1% → 3.0–3.1% in 2023–24) is a Group D composition effect, not a syllabus change.
